1. Project Overview
A cloud-based shop management platform designed to automate daily business operations. It combines inventory tracking, sales processing, expense management, basic banking/accounts, and custom report generation with print/preview options into one easy-to-use web interface.

2. Target Roles & Access Control

Admin: Full system control. Manages users, views all financial reports, exports/prints reports, and handles cash/bank accounts.

Manager: Oversees daily stock levels, approves expenses, monitors sales activities, and generates operational reports.

Sales / Staff: Uses a fast Point of Sale (POS) interface to process customer orders and print sales receipts.

3. Core Features

Stock & Inventory: Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, product categories, and barcode integration.

Sales & POS: Quick checkout, discount application, invoice generation, and receipt printing.

Expense Management: Tracking daily operational costs, bills, and staff payouts.

Accounts & Banking: Dual-ledger system separating Cash and Bank transactions with automated balance updates and Profit/Loss summaries.

Report Print & Preview (NEW):

Interactive Preview: Preview sales, inventory, expense, and profit/loss reports directly on the screen before printing or downloading.

Export & Print: Print customer invoices, daily closing summaries, and financial reports directly, or export them as clean PDF / Excel files.

4. Implementation Plan

Phase 1 (Setup): User authentication, database design, and role-based permissions.

Phase 2 (Inventory & POS): Product management, checkout terminal, and receipt print template.

Phase 3 (Finance & Reports): Expense logging, bank/cash ledgers, report preview interfaces, and PDF/Print generation.

Phase 4 (Deployment): System testing, print formatting checks across devices, and cloud launch.
